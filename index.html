      Laminate
      
    
      
      
      
      
    
      
  
      
  
      
    
      
      
      Build the hull laminate layer by layer: a base skin over the whole hull, then zones with extra layers or a foam core, and the gunwales, ribs and stringers. The designer works out the weight and checks each panel for flex (oil canning) and strength, and suggests where a core floor or ribs would help. Figures are design estimates from typical material properties, so test a panel of your real layup before you commit.
